Select the relationship by consequential observable behavior and uncertainty in the implementation, rather than convenience of encoding. State its contract evidence, valid domain, observable comparison and falsifying condition; do not assume a generic algebraic law applies without support.
Implement a small adapter against the actual supplied source and declare finite parameter domains. Choose a valid passing control. Use the common search engine to explore those domains, with at most two properties, each at most 32 total evaluations and ten seconds including control and reduction. Setup and engine commands share the original tool-call and time budget. Prefer compact aggregate output and preserve full evidence in the harness directory.
Review any returned difference against the contract, reachability and actual imported implementation before reporting it. Setup failures, invalid inputs, an unsupported oracle or exhausted finite cases do not establish a defect or general correctness. Do not silently repair an invalid property and present its earlier result as valid. If no useful supported property can be formed, disclose that limitation.
