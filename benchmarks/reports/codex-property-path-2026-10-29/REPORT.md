# Property-driven path-selection diagnostic: no demonstrated improvement

Date: 2026-10-29. Two authorized existing-case sessions delivered on their first
attempt; no failures, retries, new cases, background runs or main merge. Both arms
miss the unchanged original target and neither checks the key transformation /
consumer boundary. The preregistered investigation-path criterion **fails**.

## What the execution shows

Candidate reads contracts and familiar range/comparator paths, then explicitly selects
OR union membership and exact-version intersection before writing specifications.
Each has24 declared combinations, a passing stable-version control and a source-backed
relational oracle. Each search stops after four evaluations on a counterexample.
Union includes bounded coordinate reduction. Both adapters load the actual copied
implementation, verify its module path, and retain runtime/source provenance.
Independent offline replays reproduce both results with four evaluations each. No
invalid inputs, blocking or source-integrity failures occur in those searches.

These are useful checks: wildcard union loses an explicitly admitted prerelease;
full-range membership disagrees with exact-version intersection. Candidate additionally
confirms the Comparator construction/options failure using direct execution. All three
are previously established additional behavior classes, not the original target.
The properties are semantically supported and faithfully implemented; this is not a
bad-oracle or broken-engine result. It demonstrates autonomous property construction
and local execution on this development case.

However, both selected domains stay inside familiar OR/intersection behavior. They
contain no input family for the missing transformation boundary. Their successful
searches cannot explore a relationship absent from the chosen properties/domains.
Returning an early counterexample confirms the selected path; it does not cause the
reviewer to choose a new path. The eight local evaluations therefore do **not**
qualify investigation-path improvement. Merely increasing their sample count would
not check a relationship outside those domains.

Baseline resolves the numeric definitions in `internal/constants.js`, but likewise
never forms the distinguishing long-metadata check. It reports seven established
classes: Comparator options, OR prerelease, zero lower bound, padded caret, malformed
build acceptance, ordinary hyphen metadata, and intersection gap. Candidate reports
three, with two shared and one different intersection class. These counts are behavior
classes, not independent root-cause counts or target hits. In particular malformed
build acceptance and ordinary metadata loss cannot replace the long-tail residual-digit
target. No new independent defect class is established by this pair.

Evaluator-only original-target probes still fail against both unchanged snapshots:
the long legal metadata suffix can leave a digit that changes the parsed patch value.
Reviewers receive none of those probes. Source/contract adjudication and established
additional probes support the accepted claims. No inference that fixed sources have
no other defects is needed. Remedies are reasoned/unexecuted and are not qualified as
general fixes. Unresolved leads stay separate in `judgments.json`.

## Decision

Keep the engine experimental in draft PR16 and stop paid testing. The mechanism
works once a property is chosen, but this diagnostic provides no evidence that it
improves which relationship the reviewer investigates. The next design question is
how to derive and select useful properties/domains from actual producer-to-consumer
constraints before familiar findings consume the search slots. Do not address this
negative result by adding this case's reproduction, increasing only local samples,
or quietly tuning the prompt and re-running. No further evaluation, new case or main
promotion is authorized; propose any next paid stage and its budget separately.
